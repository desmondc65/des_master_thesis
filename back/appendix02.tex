\section{Code Availability}

The full training and evaluation code for this thesis lives in the \texttt{Stormcast-Distilation} repository. The load-bearing entry points are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{stormcast/train.py} --- regression and EDM diffusion baseline training (Hydra config selects the mode); \texttt{train\_flowcast.py} and \texttt{train\_meanflow.py} --- the two flow students.
  \item \texttt{stormcast/utils/flowcast\_loss.py} / \texttt{meanflow\_loss.py} --- the I-CFM and MeanFlow (JVP-bootstrapped, stop-gradient, adaptively weighted) losses, both sharing the per-channel weights.
  \item \texttt{stormcast/utils/flowcast\_precond.py} / \texttt{meanflow\_precond.py} --- the velocity and two-time average-velocity wrappers.
  \item \texttt{stormcast/utils/nn.py} --- \texttt{get\_preconditioned\_architecture}, \texttt{build\_network\_condition\_and\_target}, and the samplers \texttt{diffusion\_model\_forward} / \texttt{flowcast\_model\_forward} / \texttt{meanflow\_model\_forward}.
  \item \texttt{experiment\_scripts/compare\_diffusion\_vs\_flowcast.py} --- the head-to-head rollout scoreboard harness of Chapter~\ref{ch:experiments} (the MeanFlow leg is enabled with \texttt{--meanflow-checkpoint}); \texttt{flowcast\_nfe\_sweep.py} --- the NFE-versus-quality sweep of Section~\ref{sec:exp-nfe}.
  \item \texttt{experiment\_scripts/make\_thesis\_figures.py} / \texttt{make\_thesis\_qualitative.sh} --- regenerate every result figure of Chapter~\ref{ch:experiments}; all plotting reads its colours from \texttt{thesis\_style.py} so the figure set stays on one palette.
\end{itemize}

\section{Dataset and Checkpoints}

\paragraph{Zarr store.} The cleaned Taiwan RWRF dataset used for the reported experiments is the local Zarr store
\begin{center}
\texttt{exp\_3\_\ldots\_full\_cleaned\_4\_27\_2026/}
\end{center}
with sub-groups \texttt{LowRes/}, \texttt{HighRes/}, and \texttt{invariants/}, a $192\times96$ grid, and \texttt{log1p}-encoded \texttt{qpepre} (Section~\ref{sec:method-data}).

\paragraph{Checkpoints (cleaned, log1p, $\approx 2\,\text{M}$-sample anchor).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Regression mean: \texttt{StormCastUNet.0.8000.mdlus} (shared by all three heads).
  \item EDM diffusion baseline: the $\approx 2\,\text{M}$-training-sample \texttt{EDMPrecond} checkpoint.
  \item FlowCast: the $\approx 2\,\text{M}$-training-sample \texttt{FlowCastPrecond} checkpoint; the sibling \texttt{ema\_state.pt} holds the EMA shadow used by the in-training validation sampler. The canonical FlowCast run continues to the $\approx 13.4\,\text{M}$-sample checkpoint.
  \item MeanFlow: the $\approx 2\,\text{M}$-training-sample \texttt{MeanFlowPrecond} checkpoint (batch 112) with its sibling \texttt{ema\_state.pt}. The Chapter~\ref{ch:experiments} harness loads the FlowCast and MeanFlow online checkpoints symmetrically.
\end{itemize}

\section{Preprocessing Pipeline}
\label{app:preprocessing}

The pipeline that assembles the Zarr store is described in Section~\ref{sec:method-datasources-pipeline}; the two staged scripts live under \texttt{data\_preprocessing/}: \texttt{nc\_month\_to\_day/} splits monthly ERA5 NetCDF files into single-hour files, and \texttt{nc\_to\_zarr/} (driven by \texttt{config.yaml}) builds the file indices, precomputes the joint-validity mask, extracts and re-grids each source onto the shared Taiwan grid, accumulates single-pass (Welford) per-channel statistics, and writes a consolidated StormCast-style Zarr store whose on-disk layout is recorded in \texttt{zarr\_metadata.json} and consumed by \texttt{data\_loader\_rwrf\_era5\_stable.Dataset}.

\section{Commands}

All commands launch from the \texttt{stormcast/} directory. The shipped configs point at stale cluster paths and outdated \texttt{valid\_dates}; override \texttt{dataset.location}, the regression weights, and \texttt{dataset.valid\_dates=['2022/01/01','2022/12/31']} on the local workstation. Each head is trained by its entry point (\texttt{train.py} for the regression mean and, with \texttt{--config-name=diffusion}, the EDM baseline; \texttt{train\_flowcast.py}; \texttt{train\_meanflow.py}), passing \texttt{model.regression\_weights} and \texttt{dataset.location}; re-running the same command auto-resumes from the latest checkpoint plus \texttt{ema\_state.pt}.

The head-to-head scoreboard, per-threshold, FSS, CRPS, rollout, and qualitative-panel outputs of Chapter~\ref{ch:experiments} are produced by
\begin{verbatim}
bash experiment_scripts/run_main_experiment.sh
\end{verbatim}
which runs \texttt{compare\_diffusion\_vs\_flowcast.py} (MeanFlow leg at \texttt{MEANFLOW\_NFES="1 2"}) and writes \texttt{scoreboard.\{md,csv\}}, \texttt{per\_threshold.csv}, \texttt{fss\_p16.csv}, \texttt{rmse\_per\_step.csv}, and \texttt{crps\_per\_channel.csv} in physical units (qpepre in mm\,h$^{-1}$ after inverse-\texttt{log1p}). The NFE-versus-quality sweeps of Section~\ref{sec:exp-nfe} are wrapped by \texttt{run\_flowcast\_nfe\_sweep.sh} (\texttt{METHODS="flowcast meanflow"}), and every result figure is regenerated by
\begin{verbatim}
bash experiment_scripts/make_thesis_figures.sh      # CPU only
bash experiment_scripts/make_thesis_qualitative.sh  # one GPU, ~2 min
\end{verbatim}
The matched $\approx 2\,\text{M}$-sample checkpoints must be passed explicitly to the sweep, as its FlowCast default points at the longer-trained $\approx 13.4\,\text{M}$-sample checkpoint, which is not comparable to the Chapter~\ref{ch:experiments} scoreboard.
