\begin{abstract}

近年來，基於擴散模型的生成式天氣預報（例如 GenCast 與 StormCast）已展現出在機率性、銳利化、且可集成的短時預報任務上超越傳統確定性深度學習模型的能力。然而，擴散模型在推論階段需要沿著機率流常微分方程（PF-ODE）執行數十次神經網路前向傳遞（Number of Function Evaluations, NFE），使其在進行長時間自迴歸滾動預測或大規模集成預報時的計算成本過高；同時，擴散殘差在稀疏、重尾的降水通道上仍偏向平滑，難以重現對流尺度的極端降水結構。

本論文以一個以臺灣區域（RWRF）資料集訓練的兩階段 StormCast 模型（凍結的確定性回歸均值加上生成式殘差頭）為基礎，提出並評估 \textbf{FlowCast}：一個以條件流匹配（Conditional Flow Matching, CFM）為核心、\emph{無需教師、僅需單一訓練階段}的殘差生成模型。FlowCast 透過迴歸一條由雜訊到資料的直線速度場 $v_\theta$，在推論時僅以少數幾步顯式 Euler 積分即可取樣，與需要 35 NFE 的 EDM 擴散模型（保留為對照基準）形成對比。我們進一步引入 \textbf{MeanFlow}（平均速度流匹配）作為第二個同樣無需教師的殘差頭：網路直接學習整段時間區間上的\emph{平均}速度 $u_\theta$，單次前向傳遞即可橫越整段區間，推論僅需 1--2 次網路評估、完全不需數值積分；MeanFlow 與 FlowCast 共用相同的骨幹網路、損失設計與訓練預算，使兩者構成僅針對訓練目標本身的對照實驗。針對臺灣 RWRF 目標僅有 4 個高解析度輸出通道（\texttt{t2m}, \texttt{u10}, \texttt{v10}, \texttt{qpepre}）的特性，我們設計逐通道加權的 L2 損失，並在稀疏重尾的降水通道 \texttt{qpepre} 上加入徑向對數功率譜密度（log-PSD）正則化項；我們另外比較了 \texttt{log1p} 降水編碼與原始 mm/h 編碼，以及降水通道權重 $w_{\text{pr}}$ 的取捨。

我們在 2022 年整年逐小時驗證資料上，以確定性誤差（RMSE/MAE）、機率分數（CRPS）、降水類別分數（CSI、FSS、HSS、誤報率）、徑向功率譜，以及最長 12 小時的自迴歸滾動誤差等指標，將兩個流匹配殘差頭與 EDM 擴散基準進行同訓練樣本預算下的三方正面比較。實驗結果顯示，在約四分之一的推論時間下，FlowCast 在 CRPS、風場 RMSE、降水 RMSE 與誤報率上即可達到或優於 EDM 擴散；MeanFlow 更僅以 1--2 次網路評估（約為擴散取樣器十分之一的成本）即可達到同級的確定性與類別技能，並取得全研究最佳的溫度精度；其單步降水 CRPS 優於 FlowCast 在掃描範圍內任何步數的數值，唯集成離散度偏窄，在自迴歸滾動下表現為小幅的 CRPS 落差（於僅 1 次評估時最為明顯，第 2 次評估可補回大部分離散度）。本研究為區域對流尺度生成式預報提供了兩套可重現的單階段流匹配流程（直線速度與平均速度）與降水導向的評估基準。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}

Diffusion-based generative weather forecasters such as GenCast and StormCast have recently surpassed deterministic deep-learning baselines on probabilistic, sharp, and ensemble-capable short-range prediction tasks. However, sampling a diffusion model requires integrating its probability-flow ODE with tens of neural-network function evaluations (NFE) per forecast step, which makes autoregressive rollouts and large ensembles computationally prohibitive for operational regional, convective-scale deployment; moreover, the diffusion residual remains comparatively smooth on the sparse, heavy-tailed precipitation channel, where convective-scale extremes matter most.

This thesis builds on a two-stage StormCast model trained on a Taiwan-domain RWRF dataset --- a frozen deterministic regression mean plus a generative residual head --- and proposes and evaluates \textbf{FlowCast}: a residual generator based on \emph{Conditional Flow Matching} (CFM) that is \emph{teacher-free and trained in a single stage}. FlowCast regresses a straight-line noise-to-data velocity field $v_\theta$ and, at inference, samples by integrating it with only a handful of explicit Euler steps --- a direct, single-training-run alternative to the 35-NFE EDM diffusion model that we retain as the reference baseline. We further adapt \textbf{MeanFlow}, an \emph{average-velocity} flow-matching objective, as a second, equally teacher-free residual head: the network learns the mean velocity over a whole time interval, so a single forward pass transports the state across it and sampling needs only one to two network evaluations, with no numerical integration at all. MeanFlow shares FlowCast's backbone, losses, and training budget exactly, making the two flow heads an A/B test on the training objective alone. Because the Taiwan RWRF target contains only four high-resolution output channels (\texttt{t2m}, \texttt{u10}, \texttt{v10}, \texttt{qpepre}), we can afford channel-specific loss design: a channel-weighted L2 objective plus a radial log power-spectral-density (log-PSD) regularizer on the sparse, heavy-tailed precipitation channel \texttt{qpepre}. We additionally study the \texttt{log1p} versus raw-mm/h precipitation encoding and the precipitation channel-weight $w_{\text{pr}}$.

We compare both flow heads head-to-head against the EDM diffusion baseline at a matched training-sample budget on the full 2022 hourly validation year, using a precipitation-aware metric suite: per-channel RMSE/MAE, probabilistic CRPS, categorical precipitation scores (CSI, FSS, HSS, false-alarm ratio) at operational thresholds, radial log-PSD, and autoregressive rollout error out to 12 hours. Our experiments show that, at roughly a quarter of the inference wall-clock time, FlowCast matches or beats EDM diffusion on CRPS, wind-field RMSE, precipitation RMSE, and false-alarm ratio, and offers a better NFE/time-versus-quality trade-off. MeanFlow reaches the same deterministic and categorical skill class at one to two network evaluations --- roughly a tenth of the diffusion sampler's cost --- with the best temperature accuracy in the study and a single-step precipitation CRPS that undercuts FlowCast at any step count, at the price of narrowed ensemble spread that surfaces as a modest rollout-CRPS gap (largest when exactly one evaluation is used). The thesis contributes two reproducible single-run flow-matching pipelines (straight-line and average-velocity) and an evaluation protocol for regional convective-scale generative forecasting.

\end{abstract*}
